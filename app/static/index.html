01 — About

Pixels carry no scale. A floor-plan network gives you blurry masks, not walls. We fuse learned detection with classical geometry, read every dimension written on the drawing, and solve for the one scale that makes it all agree. The result is a measured building you can walk through.

02 — Process

Five steps,
zero guesswork.

Scroll →

From pixels to a building.

A hybrid of the CubiCasa5K network and classical geometry. Nothing is hidden: you can inspect every intermediate stage.

01

Ingest

PNG, JPG or the first page of a PDF is loaded and checked by content, not just its extension, then binarised so that ink is separated from paper.

02

Detect

The learned network gives room, wall and icon probabilities. Morphological ink extraction keeps edges pixel-exact and catches hollow walls.

03

Vectorise

Walls become centrelines with a thickness and rooms become clean polygons. Doors and windows are snapped to their host walls.

04

Measure

OCR reads dimension strings and room areas. Each one votes for a scale, and a constraint solver makes the walls agree with the written numbers.

05

Extrude

Walls rise to your chosen height. Orbit, walk through in first person with wall collision, or export to Blender and game engines.

04 — Technology

Measured,
not just modelled.

T/01

Constraint solver

Wall positions, extents and thicknesses, plus one global log-scale, are fitted under a Huber loss.

min Σ ρ(ri/σi) r ∈ {fidelity, junctions, OCR spans, areas}
T/02

Scale by vote

Every dimension string proposes metres-per-pixel hypotheses. The scale is the peak of a log-space density, accepted only when two independent annotations agree.

T/03

Hybrid detection

CubiCasa5K segmentation intersected with Otsu ink and morphological opening: learned semantics with classical precision.

T/04

Room topology

Enclosed space is split only along rays from reflex corners where room types change. Rectangles are never cut, and virtual boundaries stay straight.

T/05

Open by default

GLB, OBJ + MTL, layout.json and a self-contained viewer.html. Every stage image and every fallback that fired is included.

1 Choose a plan or video

Drop a plan or video here Floor plan: PNG, JPG/JPEG or PDF · up to 25 MB. Room video: MP4/MOV · up to 300 MB

Accepted formats

.png.jpg.jpeg.pdf.mp4.mov
  • PDF: the first page is used.
  • Maximum size 25 MB (videos 300 MB).
  • HEIC, TIFF, DWG and SVG are not supported. Export to PNG or PDF first.

For best results

  • A top-down architectural floor plan: a scan, export or screenshot, not a photo taken at an angle.
  • At least 800 px on the long side; one floor per image works best.
  • Dark walls on a light background.

Real-world size

  • Dimension text such as 3600, 3.6 m, 12'6", 3.4 x 4.1 m or room areas (12.5 m²) lets it measure true size.
  • Without them, size is estimated from door widths (≈ 0.78 m).
